Nepali Blood Donors

A digital blood donor network for urgent support.

The initiative helps connect patients, hospitals, volunteers, and blood donors through community coordination and mobile technology.

How it works

Simple coordination for urgent blood needs.

Nepali Blood Donors is built around fast donor discovery, request sharing, volunteer support, and reliable communication when time matters.
