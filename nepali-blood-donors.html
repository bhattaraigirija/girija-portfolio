Nepali Blood Donors

Blood donor network and emergency support.

How it works

Simple coordination for urgent blood needs.

Nepali Blood Donors is built around fast donor discovery, request sharing, volunteer support, and reliable communication when time matters.

Donor registration Blood request sharing Location-based support Emergency notifications Hospital coordination Volunteer network

Branches & Growth

Local roots with international direction.

National

Lamjung Blood Donors

The national branch strengthens donor coordination, local volunteer response, awareness activity, and community-level support in Lamjung.

International

Bangladesh (Blood Bangladesh)

The Bangladesh connection supports cross-border awareness, collaboration, and shared learning through Blood Bangladesh.

Coming Soon

South Sudan

South Sudan is planned as a future international branch, with the goal of expanding life-saving donor coordination to more communities.

Timeline

History and development.

2020

Mission started

Nepali Blood Donors began with a simple goal: make blood donor coordination easier and faster for people in urgent need.

2021

Digital coordination improved

Online presence and organized communication helped share blood requests, donor details, and emergency support more clearly.

2022

Volunteer support expanded

The initiative strengthened community response through local contacts, awareness activity, and donor follow-up.

2023

Platform development continued

App and service ideas grew around donor search, emergency blood requests, ambulance information, blood banks, and event support.

2024

Branch and collaboration focus

Lamjung Blood Donors strengthened national branch direction while Bangladesh (Blood Bangladesh) supported international collaboration.

Present

National and international growth

The network continues donor coordination in Nepal, maintains Bangladesh collaboration, and plans South Sudan as a future expansion.
