Nepali Blood Donors

Blood donor network and emergency support.

How it works

Simple coordination for urgent blood needs.

Nepali Blood Donors is built around fast donor discovery, request sharing, volunteer support, and reliable communication when time matters.

Timeline

History and development.

2020

Mission started

Nepali Blood Donors began with a simple vision: to make blood donor coordination faster, easier, and more reliable during emergencies.

June 2021

First digital release

The first version of the Nepali Blood Donors platform was launched, enabling basic donor search and emergency blood request coordination.

2022

Community and volunteer growth

The network expanded through volunteers, improving response time, donor engagement, and awareness activities across different regions.

2023

Platform and feature expansion

New ideas and features were introduced including ambulance support, blood bank listings, event coordination, and improved donor tracking.

2024

System improvement & scaling

Focus shifted towards improving system reliability, user experience, and preparing the platform for larger-scale coordination.

2025

Lamjung branch established

The Lamjung Blood Donors branch strengthened regional coordination, making local blood support faster and more organized.

2026

International expansion – Bangladesh

Blood Bangladesh was launched as an international extension, expanding the mission beyond Nepal and supporting nationwide donor coordination in Bangladesh.

Upcoming

Future expansion – South Sudan

Plans are underway to expand into South Sudan, continuing the vision of building a global blood donor support network.
